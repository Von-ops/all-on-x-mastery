How to use on phone / iPad

  1. Open this folder via a local server (see README) or AirDrop/Files.
  2. Add this site to your Home Screen (Safari Share → Add to Home Screen) for app-like access.
  3. Mark sections done as you go — progress saves in your browser (localStorage).
  4. Print or PDF the study sheets (Week 1 · Week 2) before surgery days.
