One-line definitions

Immediate loading (ITI)
Prosthesis in occlusion within 1 week of implant placement.
Primary stability
Mechanical grip at placement; gatekeeper for immediate load (torque / ISQ / clinical feel).
All-on-4 pattern
2 anterior axial + 2 posterior tilted; maximize A-P spread; often graft-minimizing.
All-on-XYZ
Plan implants (X) + bone (Y) + soft tissue (Z) from the prosthetic endpoint.
FP1
Replaces crowns only; natural soft tissue; rare in atrophic full arch.
FP2
Longer clinical crowns / partial root form; little or no pink.
FP3
Teeth + prosthetic gingiva; typical All-on-X hybrid after atrophy/reduction.
Transition zone
Junction of prosthesis and tissue—must be hidden at high smile or managed with pink/reduction.

Immediate load checklist (pocket)

  • ☐ Patient goals match fixed immediate path
  • ☐ Medical / airway / parafunction screened
  • ☐ CBCT: ≥4 sites, A-P spread, density workable
  • ☐ Restorative space & smile line → FP class chosen
  • ☐ Soft tissue / hygiene access planned
  • ☐ Expect torque ≥30–35 Ncm (confirm each implant)
  • ☐ One-piece provisional / conversion ready
  • ☐ Soft diet + occlusal scheme explained
  • ☐ Backup plan if stability fails (don’t load / stage / OD)

Path chooser

  • All-on-X: fixed desire + bone + stability + team ready
  • Overdenture: accepts removable / limited bone / cost / your locator strength
  • Staged: graft needed, soft bone expected, medical optimization, complex occlusion first

GO / CAUTION / NO — memory hooks

GO: healthy-ish, good bone, hidden transition, realistic, lab ready.

CAUTION: smoker, controlled systemic Dz, high smile, borderline bone, brux mild–mod, opposing natural.

NO (today): uncontrolled medical, severe untreated OSA + deep sedation ask, extreme brux + noncompliant hygiene, no stability possible, magical thinking after education.

Numbers worth remembering

  • ITI study inclusion often: torque ≥30 Ncm, ISQ ≥60
  • Immediate load window: ≤7 days (often same day)
  • Long-term All-on-4 survival: high 90%s range in major series (see module refs)
  • 4 vs 6 maxilla: survival similar; watch MBL/complications & patient risk
