Hygiene, complications & first-case readiness — study sheet

Kevon Rennie · All-on-X mastery · Aligns with Pikos All-on-XYZ / SmartOnX concepts · Not IFU

Week 6 in one line

Cleansable design → teach home care on delivery day → baseline x-ray + probing at delivery → recall ~5–6 mo (shorter for smokers / perio history / prior complication) → same visit order every time → diagnose by the 2017 definitions → triage, fix, and write down the cause → every readiness box checked before case 1.

Why maintain (Papaspyridakos 2012)

  • 24.6 complications per 100 restoration-years
  • Complication-free: 29.3% at 5 y · 8.6% at 10 y
  • Veneer chipping 33.3% / 66.6% · bone loss >2 mm 20.1% / 40.3% · tissue overgrowth 13% / 26% · screw fracture 10.4% / 20.8% (5 y / 10 y)
  • Lifelong, customized professional recall (Bidra 2016)

Home-care kit

  • ☐ Soft or powered brush
  • ☐ Water flosser (81.8% vs 33.3% implants with less bleeding vs floss, Magnuson 2013)
  • ☐ Interdental brushes where design allows
  • ☐ Threader / tufted floss around each abutment
  • ☐ Taught in a mirror with the patient's own hands · match aids to dexterity

Case definitions (2017 World Workshop)

Health
No redness, swelling, profuse BOP, suppuration · no further bone loss after initial healing
Mucositis
BOP (line/drop within 30 s) ± redness/swelling/suppuration · no extra bone loss · reversible (Heitz-Mayfield 2018)
Peri-implantitis
Inflammation + radiographic bone loss after healing + increasing PD vs baseline
No baseline
Bone level ≥3 mm + BOP + PD ≥6 mm (Renvert 2018)
Probing
No "healthy" PD number — track change from baseline (Berglundh 2018)

Recall visit order

  • 1 Ask · 2 Look + gentle probe · 3 X-ray vs baseline
  • 4 Remove if indicated (inflammation, access, mechanical signs, scheduled)
  • 5 Clean · screws per IFU · passive seat · torque per IFU · reseal
  • 6 Occlusion + night guard · re-coach · record · book next

Complication triage

Urgent (same day)
Spreading swelling/fever · uncontrolled bleeding · new/worse numbness · broken or loose provisional during healing · sinus symptoms
Biological
Mucositis: biofilm removal + access + coaching · peri-implantitis: non-surgical first, then treat or refer (EFP S3, Herrera 2023)
Mechanical
Remove · inspect · x-ray · find cause (misfit, occlusion, cantilever, parafunction) · repair (Maló 2019: 36.7% mechanical)
Patient
Speech, esthetics, food trapping, cleaning · adjust provisional first, carry to final

Risk → shorter recall

  • Smoking (bone loss >3 mm OR 2.72) · prior biological complication (failure HR 4.43) — Maló 2019
  • History of periodontitis — Monje 2016
  • Bruxism: RR 2.93 for failure, low-quality evidence (Chrcanovic 2015) → short cantilevers, strong material, night guard

Case 1 readiness

  • ☐ W1 straightforward patient · consent covers lifelong maintenance + repair costs
  • ☐ W2 records · teeth-first plan · space · A-P spread · written torque/ISQ thresholds · medical + sedation
  • ☐ W3 team · tray · time-out · plan B · sedation window
  • ☐ W4 verified denture · duplicate · bite index · conversion kit
  • ☐ W5 lab hand-off · scan bodies · analog backup · verification plan
  • ☐ W6 post-op visits · baseline records · home-care kit · recall · after-hours path · mentor reachable
  • Any box empty → move the date, not the standard
