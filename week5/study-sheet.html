Digital workflow & same-day prosthesis — study sheet

Kevon Rennie · All-on-X mastery · Aligns with Pikos All-on-XYZ / SmartOnX concepts · Not IFU

The digital workflow in one line

Records at approved VDO → tooth arrangement → plan implants from the teeth → guides + provisional file → surgery: MUAs → capture positions (IOS or PG) + tissue + bite → design → print 0° / mill → bond passive → seat + x-ray → torque per IFU → 8–12+ wk: reverse-scan provisional → capture + verify (jig / prototype) → final. Backup always: Week 4 pickup.

Planning records

  • ☐ CBCT (lips retracted · radiographic guide/scanned denture if few landmarks)
  • ☐ Arch / denture scans (in mouth + denture extraorally)
  • ☐ Bite at verified VDO
  • ☐ Photos: full smile · rest · retracted · profile
  • ☐ Digital tooth arrangement approved (printed try-in if possible)

Capturing implant positions

Splinted open tray
Reference standard · verified stone cast
IOS + scan bodies
~88 µm vs conventional in 36 jaws (Papaspyridakos 2023) · protocol-dependent (ITI 2018) · stitching error across edentulous arch
Photogrammetry
Better trueness/precision than IOS (Pozzi 2025; Altalla 2026) · positions only → still scan tissue + bite · still try in a rigid prototype
Reverse scan
Provisional or jig on analogs · only if it fits passively (Rosmaninho 2024)
Working thresholds
~150 µm acceptable scan discrepancy (Revilla-León 2026) · >~1° angular threatens passive fit (Pozzi 2025)

Full-arch scan technique

  • Correct scan bodies for MUA platform + lab library · fully seated
  • Dry, retracted field · steady path per scanner maker
  • Separate scans: scan bodies · tissue · opposing · bite
  • Check for holes / doubled surfaces before patient leaves · label files

Same-day provisional

  • Milled PMMA: fewer mechanical complications than printed (10% vs 45%, Vasquez-Ramos 2026)
  • Printing: validated printer + resin + post-cure · 0°, no bar (Sonderegger 2026) · settings change accuracy (Tahayeri 2018)
  • Bulk at cylinders/connectors · short cantilevers · convex intaglio (Week 4)
  • ☐ Denture, duplicate, bite index in the room — never extend sedation for a print

Lab Rx

  • ☐ Files: positions (system + scan body) · tissue · opposing · bite · pre-op/provisional · CBCT/plan
  • ☐ Photos: smile · rest · retracted · profile · provisional in · shade tab
  • ☐ MUA platform + screw · prototype/try-in/final + material · mould/shade/pink
  • ☐ Occlusion scheme · cantilever limit in mm · cleansable intaglio · changes vs provisional · due date

Verify before the final

  • No single test is objective — combine jig, one-screw, screw resistance, radiographs (Kan 1999)
  • Milled verification devices > printed (Asavanant 2025)
  • Rigid prototype try-in: fit · esthetics · phonetics · occlusion
  • CAD/CAM frameworks most consistent fit (Abduo 2011)

Definitive material

Monolithic zirconia
1.4% failure · chipping of veneer = main complication → monolithic / gingival-only veneer (Bidra 2017)
Metal-acrylic
Repairable · tooth wear/fracture common long term (Week 4)
Metal-zirconia
Less wear + peri-implantitis than metal-acrylic; similar survival (Marinkovic 2026, low-certainty)
Rule
The final is a stronger copy of the provisional that worked

First-case ladder

  • 1–3: digital plan + guides · Week 4 analog conversion · lab final with verification
  • Then: IOS records + scan bodies + jig
  • Then: same-day digital provisionals
  • Then: PG / in-house printing when volume justifies
