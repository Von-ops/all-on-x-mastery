Surgical planning & armamentarium — study sheet

Kevon Rennie · All-on-X mastery · Aligns with Pikos All-on-XYZ / SmartOnX concepts · Not IFU

Planning order

Teeth → face → space → bone → implants → MUAs → guide → tray. Never start with the bone.

One-line definitions

Prosthetic (restorative) space
Vertical room from reduced bone/tissue to occlusal plane; ≈15+ mm for hybrid, ≈12–15 mm min for zirconia (lab-specific).
Transition line
Prosthesis–tissue junction; must hide behind the lip at full smile.
Bone reduction
Only for material space + hidden transition (+ flat platform). Measured, guided, irreversible.
A-P spread
Line through most anterior implants → line through distal edge of most posterior platforms.
Cantilever rule
Teaching rule ≤ ~1.5–2 × A-P spread (dense mandible); shorter in maxilla / soft bone / bruxer; provisional shorter still.
Tilted posterior
~30–35° (up to ~45°) along anterior sinus wall or over mental loop; platform ~2nd premolar.
MUA
0° anterior · 17°/30° tilted; collar = tissue height; channels exit lingual/occlusal; torque per IFU.
Bedrossian zones (maxilla)
Z1 canine–canine · Z2 premolar · Z3 molar. Z1+Z2 only → tilted concept; Z1 only → graft / zygoma (refer).

CBCT read (pocket)

  • ☐ Prosthetic info in scan (dual scan / merged set-up)
  • ☐ Maxilla: sinus anterior wall & pathology, nasal floor, incisive canal, density
  • ☐ Mandible: mental foramen + anterior loop measured, IAN, lingual concavity
  • ☐ ≥2 mm safety margin to vital structures (static guide error ~1.2 mm entry / 1.4 mm apex / 3.5°)

Go / no-go numbers

  • Insertion torque ≥30–35 Ncm per implant (tilted often 35–45)
  • ISQ ≥60–65 (adjunct)
  • One-piece rigid cross-arch provisional, passive fit
  • Plan B written + consented: drop/add an implant · or removable interim

Medical / pharm / sedation

  • Antiresorptive: oncologic-dose IV BP / denosumab → no elective implants; osteoporosis dose → consent, minimize trauma (AAOMS 2022)
  • Diabetes: controlled ≈ normal survival; poorly controlled → more peri-implantitis/failure; many defer above ~8% HbA1c
  • Smoking: ↑ failure, infection, MBL — CAUTION
  • Anticoagulants: follow guidance + prescriber; local hemostasis; don't stop on your own
  • Sedation: permit level, ASA, airway/STOP-BANG, monitoring incl. capnography, escort, NPO (ADA 2016)

Tray (don't forget)

  • Reduction burs / piezo · reduction guide + pins
  • Implants: planned + backup lengths/diameters · motor with torque readout · ISQ
  • MUAs all angles/heights + spares · MUA drivers · torque wrench
  • Temporary cylinders · healing caps · spare screws
  • Conversion: denture/printed provisional · dam squares · pickup resin · burs · polish
  • Sutures · hemostatics · PRF (optional) · radiograph to verify MUA seating
