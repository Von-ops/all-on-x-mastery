Smart Denture Conversion 2.0 — study sheet

Kevon Rennie · All-on-X mastery · Aligns with Pikos All-on-XYZ / SmartOnX concepts · Not IFU

The conversion in one line

Go/no-go = LOAD → caps on → locate + hollow → cylinders + passive try-in at bite-index VDO → isolate → pick up in occlusion → analogs: fill, de-flange, convex intaglio, cut cantilevers, polish → seat + x-ray → torque per IFU → occlusion → PTFE + composite → instruct.

Before surgery day

  • ☐ Denture verified: VDO · esthetics/midline · occlusion · enough acrylic bulk · teeth bond to pickup resin
  • ☐ Transition line hidden after planned reduction (Week 2)
  • ☐ Duplicate made · bite index at approved VDO · midline/plane/MUA positions marked
  • ☐ Reinforcement decision (bruxer, natural opposing, thin denture)
  • ☐ Mock conversion on a model with your real components, timed

Tray

  • Temporary cylinders + prosthetic screws (spares) · MUA caps · MUA analogs · driver · restorative torque wrench
  • Dam squares · PTFE · block-out · separator per IFU
  • Low-exotherm pickup resin · flowable touch-up · lab handpiece, burs, discs · pumice + polish
  • Articulating paper/foil · Miller forceps · x-ray sensor · water-flosser tip / threader

Pickup rules

  • Over-relieve; no cylinder touches the denture before resin
  • Full seat at bite-index VDO · midline matches photos
  • Isolate: dam to tissue, block channels/undercuts — no resin under tissue or below MUA
  • Small resin volume · patient held closed · cool · full set time
  • Doesn't seat / rocks → stop and relieve. Never force.

Design numbers

Cantilever
Interim acrylic: mean ~9–10 mm, CL/AP ≈ 0.5–0.6 did well (Drago 2017) · usually end at 1st molar / 2nd premolar · shorter vs natural opposing, bruxers, borderline distal
Fracture risk
17.7% interim repairs (Drago 2017) · 17.6% complete fractures yr 1, milled PMMA (Mendonça 2024)
Tissue
Hyperplasia 13% at 5 y / 26% at 10 y around fixed full-arch (Papaspyridakos 2012) → hygienic design from day 1

Intaglio

  • No flanges · no palate (maxilla) · convex-to-flat · no ridge lap
  • Light/no pressure on swelling tissue · every void filled · high polish
  • Floss threader / water-flosser passes on analogs before delivery
  • Check lip support + F/V/S after delivery

Occlusion (Kim 2005 · Sheridan 2016)

  • Even, bilateral, simultaneous, light centric over implant-supported segment
  • Cantilevers light/out of contact · no excursive contacts on them
  • Shallow shared anterior guidance · no working/balancing interferences
  • Narrow, flat tables · opposing denture stays seated
  • Re-check at 24 h and 1 week

Delivery

  • ☐ Hand-tighten all → cross-arch tighten → radiograph every interface
  • ☐ Prosthetic screw torque per MUA IFU (lower than abutment torque — look it up, post it)
  • ☐ PTFE + temporary composite in channels (Maluly-Proni 2017; no material clearly best — Jain 2022)
  • ☐ Photos · torque · components · occlusion notes
  • ☐ MUAs stay put — one abutment, one time (Degidi 2011)

Home & recall

  • Soft diet through integration (~8–12 wk) · no hard biting on front teeth or back ends
  • Soft brush + water flosser · call if loose, clicking, cracked, bite changes, worsening swelling
  • 24 h · 1 wk · 2–4 wk · 8–12+ wk → final (Week 5)

Don't convert today if…

  • Go/no-go said stage, or loaded implants give poor A-P spread
  • Intraoperative event still being managed
  • Denture broken with no duplicate · patient/sedation time exhausted
  • → Relieved, soft-lined removable interim; convert at a scheduled visit
