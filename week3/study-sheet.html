Surgery-day protocol — study sheet

Kevon Rennie · All-on-X mastery · Aligns with Pikos All-on-XYZ / SmartOnX concepts · Not IFU

The day in one line

Sign In → local → Time Out → extract + debride → flap + nerves → reduce + verify space → distal tilted → anterior axial → GO/NO-GO → MUAs + x-ray → graft + close → dry → Sign Out → conversion.

T-1 & morning-of

  • ☐ Consent incl. plan B (may leave removable) · questions answered
  • ☐ Meds per Week 2 review: anticoag plan w/ prescriber · diabetic plan · antiresorptive · premed filled
  • ☐ Sedation: provider · NPO · escort · cart, O2, suction ×2, AED, reversal, capnography
  • ☐ Guides test-fit on model · pins · conversion denture/provisional · bite
  • ☐ Implants planned + longer/wider backups · ISQ charged · motor torque readout
  • ☐ MUAs every angle/cuff + spares · drivers · torque wrench · caps · cylinders · screws
  • ☐ Team huddle: roles, sequence, plan B triggers, emergency roles
  • ☐ Morning: vitals · glucose · premed taken · plan posted · CBCT on screen · CHX rinse

WHO checklist (adapted)

Sign In (before sedation/LA)
ID · arch · consent/plan B · allergies · airway · anticoag/glucose · monitors + drugs checked
Time Out (before incision)
Roles · teeth by number · sites/sizes/tilt · MUAs · components present · imaging up · antibiotic per protocol · critical events · torque/ISQ thresholds · sterility
Sign Out (before handoff)
Lot #s, torque, ISQ · radiograph · counts + guide pins out · events noted · conversion/recovery plan
Evidence
Haynes 2009: deaths 1.5% → 0.8%, complications 11.0% → 7.0% after checklist introduction (hospital setting)

Surgical pearls

  • Debride every socket to clean bone: curette, bur, irrigate, CHX (Chrcanovic 2015). Pus/cyst you can't clean → relocate or stage.
  • Flap: mid-crestal, split keratinized tissue, full thickness; releases away from mental foramen and greater palatine region.
  • See both mental foramina; finger on the lingual plate in the anterior mandible.
  • Reduce to the guide/measured level; verify space (≈15 mm hybrid / lab spec) before drilling.
  • Sequence: distal tilted (~30–45°) first — maxilla along anterior sinus wall, mandible mesial to foramen/loop (≥2 mm) — then anterior axial.
  • Soft bone: under-prepare, longer tapered, engage cortex (Tabassum 2009). Dense bone: full sequence + tap; never exceed IFU max torque (Bashutski 2009).

Go / no-go

  • Every implant ≥ written threshold (teaching: ≥30–35 Ncm · ISQ ≥60–65 adjunct) + adequate number/A-P → GO
  • One low → rescue (longer/wider/new site) → else cover screw, exclude, load rest only if number + spread OK → CAUTION
  • 2+ low or poor spread → cover screws/healing abutments + relined removable interim → NO (stage)

MUA seating

  • ☐ Depth gauge → cuff puts platform at/just above tissue
  • ☐ Angle: straight (axial) · 17° / 30° (tilted) — system-specific
  • ☐ Clear mesial bone over tilted implants · index channel occlusal/lingual
  • ☐ Torque per manufacturer IFU (straight vs angled may differ)
  • ☐ Radiograph each interface · protection caps on · one abutment, one time

Closure · hemostasis · handoff

  • Graft gaps with reduction bone · thin flaps · KT buccal + lingual to MUAs · tension-free sutures, platforms uncovered
  • Pressure → vasoconstrictor → hemostatic agents → bone wax → sutures → TXA per plan · dry field before pickup
  • Handoff: implant/MUA map · decision (load/stage) · events · caps on · patient can close for occlusion

Complications (first move)

  • Floor-of-mouth swelling: airway first — stop, sit up, O2, bimanual pressure, 911 (Kalpidis 2004)
  • Sinus perforation: stop; nothing into sinus; stable implant → may proceed, else shorten/redirect; sinus precautions
  • Nerve proximity: stop, withdraw, shorter/reposition; neurosensory check; encroaching implant → remove early, refer
  • Plate fracture: stable → graft; unstable → relocate/stage
  • Guide won't seat: fix interference or pre-planned freehand / stage

Post-op (adapt to protocol)

  • Ice 24–48 h · head up · swelling peaks day 2–3
  • Soft, no-heavy-chewing diet through integration (commonly ~8–12 wk) · nothing on cantilevers
  • Meds per protocol · antibiotics: single 2 g amoxicillin ~1 h pre-op probably ↓ early failures (Cochrane 2026, non-augmented placement)
  • ER/call: breathing/swallowing trouble, FOM swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, fever, persistent numbness, loose prosthesis
  • Call 24 h · see ~1 wk · ~2 wk → Week 4/5 pathway
